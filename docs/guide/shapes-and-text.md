# Shapes, paths and text

Draw shapes and text that stay sharp at any size and keep their points editable. They go on a vector layer. Click **vector** in the Layers pane to add one, or just draw: while a paint layer is active, klad puts the shape on a new vector layer above it.

## Draw a rectangle or an ellipse

Press **U** and drag. Hold **shift** for a square or a circle.

## Draw a path

Press **P**, click to place corners and drag to pull out curves. Click the first point to close the path, or press **Return** to leave it open.

## Change the points

Press **A**, click the shape and drag its points and handles. Alt click on the outline adds a point.

To move several points together, drag a box around them, then drag one of them. Hold **shift** while you drag the box to take the points of every shape on the layer, not only the one you clicked.

## Line up several shapes

1. Press **V** and click the first shape.
2. Shift click the others, or drag a loop around them.
3. In the bar under them, click an align button: left, centre or right edges, or top, middle or bottom.

The two buttons with arrows space three or more shapes evenly. With one shape selected, **Edit > Align** lines it up on the canvas.

## Change the outline

Select shapes with **V**. In the bar under them, drag **outline** or type a width; 0 takes the outline off. **ends** sets flat, round or square ends and sharp, round or cut corners.

## Give shapes the look of another

1. Select the shape whose look you like with **V** and press **alt cmd C**.
2. Select the other shapes and press **alt cmd V**. They take its fill, outline and opacity.

## Join or cut shapes

1. Select two shapes or more with **V** and shift click.
2. Pick **Unite**, **Subtract**, **Intersect** or **Exclude** from the bar's combine button, or from **Edit > Shape**.

The result is one path with the look of the shape at the back. **Subtract** cuts the shapes in front out of it.

## Line things up on a grid or guides

1. Press **cmd '** to show the grid and **shift cmd '** to snap to it. **View > Grid…** sets its spacing, stronger lines and look, square or isometric.
2. Press **cmd R** for the rulers and drag a guide out of one. Drag it back onto the ruler to take it away.

While snapping is on, shapes, points, moves, the pen and bones snap to the grid, the guides and the edges and middles of other shapes. Hold **cmd** while you drag to leave the snap.

## Fill a shape with a gradient

Select the shape with **V**, then in layer settings switch its fill to **linear** or **radial** and edit the stops.

## Write text

Press **T**, click and type, and press **Esc** to finish. The font picker also finds any family on Google Fonts, and the font is kept inside the drawing. On a pixel layer the text becomes pixels when you finish it (see [pixel art](pixel-art.md)).

To turn text into shapes, select it and choose **Edit > Points > Make it a path**. Its letters become a path you can edit with **A**.

## Repeat a shape

Select it and pick **repeat it**, **scatter it** or **mirror it** from the Layer menu. The copies stay settings you can change.
