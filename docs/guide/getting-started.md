# Getting started

Make a drawing, paint on it, then change one of your strokes after you've painted it. It takes about five minutes.

Keys are written the way a Mac shows them. On Windows and Linux, cmd is ctrl.

## Get klad

Get the builds on [klad.studio](https://klad.studio/pricing), or [build klad from source](https://github.com/klad-app/klad#from-source): it is free and takes about ten minutes. The builds need:

- **Mac:** macOS 14 Sonoma or later, on Apple silicon or Intel.
- **Windows:** Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit.

On Linux, build klad from source. On a Mac and on Windows the pen gives pressure and tilt (on Windows through Windows Ink), and on Linux you draw with the mouse for now.

## Make a drawing

![The start window, with recent drawings and the samples](../images/start-window.png)

1. Start klad and press **cmd N**. The first time, a welcome comes first: it connects your agent app and offers the first steps. Skip it with **Esc**.
2. Type a name and click **Create**.

There's nothing to save: klad writes every change to the drawing as you make it. **cmd S** says so, and where the drawing is.

## Save a copy, rename or go back

- **File > Save a copy…** (**shift cmd S**) writes a copy under a new name next to the drawing. Choose **Save and go on in the copy**, or **Save, stay here**.
- **File > Rename…** gives the drawing a new name. klad goes on in it.
- **File > Duplicate** makes a copy next to it and keeps you in this one.
- **File > Revert** takes the drawing back to how it was when you opened it. **cmd Z** takes that back.

In the start window, right click a drawing to **Duplicate** it or **Move to the Trash**. You can get it back from the Trash.

## See a drawing in Finder

On a Mac, select a drawing in Finder and press **space** to see it, and icon and gallery views show its picture. klad writes that picture when you close the drawing or quit klad. A drawing your agent made without the editor shows its picture after `klad preview art.klad`. Windows and Linux show a drawing as a folder.

## Keep several drawings open

Open another drawing with **cmd O**, **cmd N** or the start window: the one you were in stays open. A tab for each open drawing shows above the canvas. Click a tab, or press **ctrl Tab** and **ctrl shift Tab**, to go between them. Strokes and shapes you copy in one drawing paste into another, with their brushes and pictures.

## Paint, then change a stroke

1. Press **B** and paint a few strokes.
2. Press **S** and click one of them.
3. In the bar next to the selection, click the palette button.
4. Pick a colour on the wheel. The stroke takes the new colour and keeps its shape and pressure.
5. Press **cmd Z** to undo that, and **cmd shift Z** to redo it.

Every stroke stays editable like this, hours later too: see [Changing strokes](selecting.md).

## Find your way around

- **cmd K** finds any command: type a word for what you want to do.
- **?** lists every key.
- Hold **space** and drag to pan, pinch or hold **cmd** and scroll to zoom, and press **F** to see the whole canvas.
- **R** and **shift R** turn the view, and so does a two-finger twist (hold **shift** for steps of 15 degrees). Click **upright** at the top of the canvas, or press **alt R**, to put it back.
- Right click the canvas for copy, paste and **Move to new layer**.

**Help > First steps** in the editor walks you through what klad does: paint a shape and erase part of it, paint and fill a second one, reshape the first stroke, and let your agent change it.
